Industrial Production

Quality Control

I worked on physicochemical and microbiological quality control of the commercial syrup, including active ingredient, finished product and purified water controls.

Research Idea

I proposed to repurpose the same antihistamine active ingredient into a topical dosage form to target symptoms of atopic dermatitis locally.

Formulation Design

HISTAGAN® Syrup

I worked on HISTAGAN® 0.01%, an oral antihistamine syrup containing dexchlorpheniramine maleate. During this project, I followed both the industrial production process and the quality control workflow of the commercial formulation at SAIDAL..

Quality Control

I performed and followed physicochemical and microbiological quality control procedures, including analytical checks of the active ingredient, finished product and purified water.

Topical Reformulation

I proposed the idea of developing a new topical dosage form using the same active pharmaceutical ingredient, with the goal of targeting symptoms associated with atopic dermatitis.

Ointment Development

I selected excipients, designed the formulation strategy, prepared the ointment and performed physicochemical quality controls such as homogeneity, pH and sterility assessment.

In Vivo Atopic Dermatitis Model

I evaluated the formulation in an experimental atopic dermatitis model induced by DNCB in mice, comparing untreated and treated conditions.

Therapeutic Evaluation

The formulation was assessed through lesion scoring and scratching behaviour, allowing evaluation of its potential effect on dermatitis symptoms.
