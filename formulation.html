PHARMACEUTICAL R&D PROJECT

Topical Antihistamine Drug Repurposing

Transforming an oral antihistamine syrup into a topical therapeutic strategy for atopic dermatitis.

Explore Project
Dexchlorpheniramine Maleate

H1 antihistamine • Drug repurposing • Topical formulation

One active pharmaceutical ingredient.
Two therapeutic strategies.

This project explored whether dexchlorpheniramine maleate, originally used in HISTAGAN® oral syrup, could be reformulated into a topical dosage form to locally target symptoms of atopic dermatitis.

PROJECT STORY

From Industrial Production to Therapeutic Evaluation

Industrial Production

Followed the manufacturing workflow of HISTAGAN® syrup in an industrial pharmaceutical environment.

Quality Control

Performed physicochemical and microbiological controls, including HPLC-based analytical evaluation.

Research Question

Can the same active pharmaceutical ingredient be reformulated into a topical dosage form for local treatment of atopic dermatitis?

In Vivo Evaluation

Evaluated the topical formulation in a DNCB-induced atopic dermatitis model.

What I Did

  • Followed the industrial manufacturing process of HISTAGAN® syrup.
  • Performed quality control of a commercial oral antihistamine formulation.
  • Used analytical and QC methods including HPLC, IR, pH, density and microbiological testing.
  • Proposed the development of a topical formulation using dexchlorpheniramine maleate.
  • Designed the formulation strategy and selected pharmaceutical excipients.
  • Prepared the ointment and performed physicochemical and microbiological controls.
  • Evaluated the formulation in an in vivo atopic dermatitis model.

What I Can Do Independently

  • Follow an industrial pharmaceutical production workflow.
  • Design a basic topical formulation strategy.
  • Select excipients according to formulation needs.
  • Prepare topical dosage forms such as ointments.
  • Perform pH, homogeneity, sterility and physicochemical controls.
  • Use and interpret HPLC-based quality control data.
  • Interpret preclinical in vivo observations and lesion scoring.
